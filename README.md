# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard — Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Transforming raw sales data into actionable business insights is essential for data-driven decision-making. This project focuses on building an **interactive Sales Performance Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), evaluate monthly sales trends, and compare product category performance.

Using a structured sample dataset, the dashboard provides a consolidated view of revenue, profit, order volume, and regional sales performance. Interactive filtering enables users to explore sales data by region and identify patterns that can support business planning.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Analytics Architecture

* **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Source:** Microsoft Excel
* **Data Preparation:** Data import and data type validation
* **Data Visualization:** KPI Cards, Column Charts, Interactive Slicers
* **Analytical Focus:** Sales Performance Monitoring, Profit Analysis, Category Comparison, Regional Filtering

### Core Dataset Structure

The `Sales_Data` dataset contains 15 sample sales records and seven attributes:

1. `OrderID`: Unique identifier for each order.
2. `OrderDate`: Date on which the order was recorded.
3. `Product`: Product purchased.
4. `Category`: Product classification, including Electronics, Furniture, and Accessories.
5. `Region`: Sales region, including North, South, East, and West.
6. `Sales`: Sales value associated with each order.
7. `Profit`: Profit generated from each order.

## 📊 Dashboard Components & Business Logic

### 1. Executive KPI Monitoring

Developed KPI cards to provide an immediate overview of business performance:

* **Total Sales:** 6,000
* **Total Profit:** 970
* **Total Orders:** 15

These metrics summarize the dataset's overall sales activity and profitability.

### 2. Monthly Sales Performance Analysis

Created a monthly column chart to compare sales performance across January, February, and March 2026.

The visualization helps identify month-to-month changes and highlights periods with higher sales activity.

### 3. Product Category Performance

Built a category-level sales comparison to evaluate the contribution of Electronics, Furniture, and Accessories.

The chart highlights the categories generating the highest sales within the sample dataset.

### 4. Interactive Regional Analysis

Implemented a region slicer to filter dashboard visuals dynamically across North, South, East, and West.

This interactive feature allows users to investigate regional differences in sales and profitability without manually modifying the underlying dataset.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

* **Category Performance:** Electronics generated the highest sales among the three product categories.
* **Monthly Trend:** Sales increased from January to February and increased again in March.
* **Profitability Overview:** The dataset records total profit of 970 against total sales of 6,000.
* **Interactive Exploration:** Regional filtering supports more focused comparisons of sales performance across different locations.

## 💼 Business Applications & Potential Impact

* **Performance Monitoring:** KPI cards provide a quick overview of sales, profit, and order volume.
* **Product Strategy:** Category comparisons can help businesses identify products that contribute most to sales.
* **Regional Planning:** Interactive filtering supports regional performance reviews and helps identify areas requiring further investigation.
* **Data-Driven Decisions:** Monthly trends provide a starting point for sales planning and performance evaluation.
